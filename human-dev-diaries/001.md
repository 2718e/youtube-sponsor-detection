## observed correlation between content position and assigned probability

Noticed after saving requests to local running laya and analysing them that the probability assigned to a particular Line being the start of the sponsor segment was **strongly** correlated with how early the line was in the list of lines.

i.e. that the relative position of the content dominated the actual content itself when it came to how probable the local model thought it was sponsored or not.

Speculation: something to do with context windows?

at any rate - don't think the local model will work with the current prompt structure and evaluation strategy.
